# KAORI Integrations Final Lock v1.1 Audit — 2026-08-08

## Decision

The Prometheus Configuration Decision side rail already matched the Connection Configuration form height, but its Controlled Flow occupied only the upper part of a large flexible section. The resulting empty region made the rail look unfinished. The refinement preserves the symmetric geometry and replaces unused capacity with compact, factual operational decision support.

## Prometheus Configuration Decision

- Connection Contract remains unchanged.
- Change Impact remains explicit and is shortened.
- Controlled Flow is presented as a compact 2x2 Configure/Validate/Observe/Resolve grid.
- Operational Effects records the consequences of Save Changes, Test Connection, Full Validation, and Consumer Use.
- Validation Boundary maps transport, capability, consumer, and operational gates to their canonical actions.
- Consumer Boundary copy is shortened while preserving Prometheus/KAORI authority separation.
- Shared macro arguments remain optional so Alertmanager is not forced to adopt Prometheus-specific evidence.
- Equal-height left/right configuration geometry remains required.
- Dark-mode token usage remains neutral-first.
