Validation & Safety

Set INTEGRATION_VAULT_ALLOWED_ORIGINS in the app and workers, then trust the approved internal CA.

  • Only HTTPS endpoints on the approved origin list are accepted.
  • AppRole credentials require a non-root, read-oriented policy.
  • Test Connection confirms AppRole identity but does not establish Full Validation readiness.
  • After Test Connection, re-enter SecretID if the password input is cleared before saving.
  • Single-use SecretIDs cannot be reused for test, save, and worker validation; use an approved reusable/rotated credential for this workflow.

Connection tests expire after 10 minutes and are bound to the exact fields and submitting user.
