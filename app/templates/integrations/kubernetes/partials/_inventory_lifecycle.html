Shared Source Boundary

Deleting this Kubernetes connection removes only its Prometheus and Alertmanager bindings. The reusable source instances and their other scope bindings remain intact.

Removed With the Connection

Kubernetes identity, stored kubeconfig, synchronized inventory, cluster-scoped governance/configuration rows, and this cluster's source bindings.

Retained After Deletion

Shared Prometheus/Alertmanager instances, generated reports and schedules, runtime observation history, JobExecution history, and Activity Audit evidence.

Connection Lifecycle

KAORI performs a governed delete only after active work and protected operational requests are resolved.
