Important Boundaries

Keep these contracts intact while moving through the setup flow.

Provider Authority

Native Kubernetes and supported Capsule clusters allow direct creation. Existing Capsule Tenant CRs use Discovery → Adoption.

Namespace Creation

Use Namespace Provisioning after Tenant creation; do not bypass profile and capacity governance.

Identity Before Access

Membership establishes the principal. Access Grant establishes the authorized profile and scope.

Credentials Last

Human and CI/CD credentials are issued only from committed, eligible access state.

Cross-Tenant references are blocked. Identity, scope, grants, and credentials stay within one Tenant.

Tenant Lifecycle Contract

Tenant identity is intentionally immutable after creation. Structural changes use governed teardown and recreation instead of inline edits.

1 · Lock Identity

Tenant name/slug, cluster placement, provider authority, and ownership boundary are not edited in place.

2 · Revoke Access

Revoke credentials and Access Grants, then retire active memberships before teardown.

3 · Remove Namespaces

Governed namespace deprovisioning must complete and provider evidence must confirm removal.

4 · Decommission

Final Tenant decommission is allowed only after Native/Capsule authority reports no managed residue.

Change strategy: recreate the Tenant after governed teardown when immutable identity or provider placement must change.
