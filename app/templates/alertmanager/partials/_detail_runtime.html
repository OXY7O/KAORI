Readiness versus Health

Readiness comes from validation. Health comes from the latest runtime observation. Both are required for reliable source use.

How Monitoring Works

Runtime refresh records reachability, latency, diagnostic stages, capability snapshot, and health score as immutable evidence.

Resolver Correlation

A bound source can be skipped when disabled, not ready, missing capabilities, or operationally unhealthy.
