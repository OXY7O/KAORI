Binding purpose

Authorizes one reusable connection for a specific operational context without duplicating endpoint or credential configuration.

Validation guardrails

The same instance cannot be bound twice to one scope. Only one enabled primary source is allowed per scope.

Resolver correlation

Role and priority establish preference; enabled state, readiness, capabilities, and runtime health determine eligibility.
