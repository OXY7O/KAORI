What a binding does

A binding authorizes this reusable connection for an operational context without duplicating endpoint configuration.

Selection order

Role and priority establish preference; enabled state, readiness, capability requirements, and runtime health establish eligibility.

Operational flow

Create binding → resolver evaluates candidates → effective source is shown → fallback is used when higher-preference sources are ineligible.
