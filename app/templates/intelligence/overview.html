Evidence Boundary

Recommendations and Alert Intelligence read committed KAORI evidence. The Overview never calls Prometheus, Thanos, Alertmanager, or Object Storage providers directly.

Alert Lifecycle

Prometheus and Alertmanager own source evaluation and raw alert state. KAORI owns acknowledgement, assignment, notification policy, delivery silence, resolution evidence, postmortem, history, and archive lifecycle.

Automation Boundary

Automation Control owns approvals, execution readiness, verification, and recovery. Stale or failed alert snapshots may remain visible to operators but cannot materialize new automation candidates.
