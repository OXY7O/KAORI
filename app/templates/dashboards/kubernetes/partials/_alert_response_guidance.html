Alert Interpretation & Response

Meaning, triage order, and controlled response steps for active Kubernetes alerts.

What This Summary Means

Alerts Summary is a committed worker snapshot of active Alertmanager signals, with Prometheus used only as the configured fallback source. Counts describe current signal severity; they do not by themselves confirm service impact or authorize remediation.

Severity Interpretation

Critical

Potential active service impact. Begin incident triage immediately and establish ownership.

Warning

Degradation or risk is developing. Investigate before thresholds escalate or capacity is exhausted.

Info

Operational context or low-risk signal. Validate relevance and watch for recurrence.
