Password Policy

Use strong credentials for local database-managed access.

Minimum strength

Use at least 12 characters for administrator recovery passwords. Longer passphrases are preferred.

Avoid predictable values

Do not use default, reused, username-based, or environment example passwords.

Temporary recovery use

Share the new password through a secure channel and ask the user to rotate it after login when appropriate.

LDAP and SSO accounts are managed by their external identity provider and cannot be reset from this local password recovery page.
