Operational Boundaries

Understand what this workspace governs and where related actions belong.

Governed Here

MFA assurance policy, Turnstile activation and session security posture are administered or reviewed under Access.

Challenges follow committed policy. KAORI records security activity without storing authentication tokens in audit evidence.

Managed Elsewhere

Users enroll authenticators in Settings. Primary login sources are registered under Identity Providers.

Idle/absolute session timeouts and remote session inventory remain separate qualification workstreams.
