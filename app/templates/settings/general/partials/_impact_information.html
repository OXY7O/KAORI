Storage & Execution Boundary

Presentation settings never rewrite operational source data.

Stored timestamps
Remain canonical UTC values in PostgreSQL and normalized data stores.
Relative ages
Values such as 3h, 12d, and certificate days remaining are timezone independent.
Schedules and external systems
Report schedules, Kubernetes, Prometheus, Alertmanager, LDAP, and OAuth provider clocks retain their own authoritative configuration.
