Policy Boundary

Upstream lifecycle, KAORI qualification, and live readiness are independent evidence layers.

General Contract
Stable API/protocol generation; this is the runtime compatibility identity persisted by KAORI.
Unverified Future Version
If mandatory contract probes pass, KAORI reports Degraded / Unverified instead of blocking solely on the version number.
Blocking Rule
Missing mandatory capabilities, invalid provider identity, or failed live validation remain blocking conditions.
